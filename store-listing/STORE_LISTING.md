**Full description** (max 4000 chars):

MedPocket is a simple, free medical pocketbook you can use anywhere, even without an internet connection.

FIRST AID
Clear, step-by-step basics for CPR, choking, severe bleeding, burns, stroke (BE FAST), heart attack warning signs, severe allergic reactions, fainting, seizures, heat stroke, low blood sugar, nosebleeds, sprains and poisoning.

VITAL SIGNS
Typical adult and children's ranges for heart rate, breathing rate, temperature, oxygen saturation and blood pressure categories.

LAB VALUES
Common adult reference ranges for a complete blood count, blood sugar and HbA1c, electrolytes and kidney tests, cholesterol and thyroid (TSH).

COMMON CONDITIONS AND HEALTHY LIVING
Short overviews of high blood pressure, type 2 diabetes, asthma, dehydration and fever, plus tips on hand washing, activity, sleep and healthy eating.

TOOLS
• BMI calculator (metric and imperial)
• Temperature converter (°C / °F)
• Blood glucose converter (mg/dL / mmol/L)
• Weight converter (kg / lb)

FEATURES
• Works 100% offline
• Fast search across all topics
• Bookmark topics for quick access
• Dark mode and adjustable text size
• Optimised for phones, tablets and foldables
• No account, no ads, no data collection, no permissions

Content is summarised from public guidance by WHO, the American Heart Association, American Red Cross, NHS, CDC, American Diabetes Association and MedlinePlus, and sources are listed on every topic.

IMPORTANT: MedPocket is for general education only. It is not medical advice, not a medical device, and not a substitute for a qualified healthcare professional. In an emergency, call your local emergency number immediately.

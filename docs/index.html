Privacy Policy

App: MedPocket – Medical Pocketbook · Effective date: 21 Nov 2026

This privacy policy explains how the MedPocket Android app ("the App"), published by Trimzi333, handles information.

1. Information we collect

The App does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal or sensitive information. It has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, crash reporting or third-party tracking services, and it does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

2. Information stored on your device

If you bookmark a topic, the list of bookmarked topic names and a flag that you have read the medical disclaimer are saved only in the App's private storage on your device. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

3. Internet access

All content is bundled within the App and works offline. The App does not send data to any server.

4. Children's privacy

The App is intended for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

5. Medical disclaimer

The App provides general health and first-aid information for educational purposes only. It is not medical advice and is not a substitute for professional diagnosis or treatment. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
